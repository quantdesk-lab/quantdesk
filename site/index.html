Formulaic alphas are research observables, not signals.

QuantDesk is a stdlib-only Python library that evaluates 19 adapted Alpha101 expressions and a vol-scaled time-series momentum stack on your own bars, then refuses to print any statistic its sample cannot support. This page is the library running on synthetic geometric-Brownian-motion data at build time: the pipeline is real, the numbers are deliberately meaningless.
